# TAG Agency Website Audit

Source audited: https://www.tagagency.in/

## Verified Information From Existing Website

- Business name: TAG Agency.
- The website describes Facebook and Instagram advertising services.
- The website describes Google Advertising Services.
- The website states that Meta ads and Google ads are the agency's sole focus.
- The website references ad account audits, audience research, testing, optimisation, funnels, product photography and video production.
- The website displays a portfolio logo area with names/images including Ambika, BM, Moon maternity, MUDA, DIC Gov, WCD, BJP, Panchamrutha, Congress Logo and SVT Gold.
- The website displays this address: `#1117, 2nd Floor, 5th Gate, Manyata Tech Park, Bengaluru North – 560077`.
- The website displays a case-study block with leads, ad spend and cost per lead, but the ad spend formatting is unclear.

## Weaknesses Observed

- Generic headline and template-like structure.
- Unclear conversion path and weak lead qualification.
- Case-study figures appear without enough context or publication verification.
- Portfolio permissions are unclear, especially political organisation logos.
- No visible structured qualification form on the audited page.
- Content repeats broad agency claims without a strong performance-marketing decision narrative.

## Items Requiring Confirmation

- Case study numbers and spend formatting.
- Approved client logos and permissions.
- Contact information.
- Address.
- Social links.
- Creative Production service status.
- Testimonials or case-study permissions.
- Political organisation logo permissions, if displayed.
